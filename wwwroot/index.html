Market Differentiation

How NayanCare is different from other softwares in the market

Generic hospital software forces eye clinics into clumsy, one-size-fits-all templates. NayanCare is purpose-built from day one exclusively for ophthalmic workflows.

Ophthalmology-First Clinical Workflows

Generic HMS forces doctors into plain text boxes. NayanCare features dedicated OD/OS refraction charts, autorefractometer data import, slit-lamp templates, IOP tonometry, and diabetic retinopathy records in the primary consultation view.

Direct Doctor-to-Optical Workflow

In most hospitals, the optical shop runs disconnected POS software requiring double entry. NayanCare automatically passes digital optical prescriptions to your optical counter in real-time, eliminating handwriting errors.

Cataract & Surgical Counselling Pipeline

General software has no concept of eye surgery counselling. NayanCare tracks surgical advice, IOL lens choices (Monofocal, Toric, Multifocal), patient package financing, and post-op review milestones.

Specialized Eye Pharmacy & Batch Tracking

Built for ophthalmic formulations — eye drops, single-strip blister packs, surgical consumables, and ointments. Automated expiry warnings prevent dispensing expired medicines with one-click prescription filling.

Automated Vision Recall & Ocular CRM

Eye care relies heavily on repeat visits: annual vision checks, post-cataract reviews, glaucoma pressure monitoring, and diabetic eye exams. Automated WhatsApp/SMS alerts keep patient retention above 90%.

100% Cloud-Native with Zero Server IT Overhead

No expensive local server hardware, no AMC contracts, and no software installations. Runs instantly in any modern browser across desktops, tablets, and phones with automated daily backups.
